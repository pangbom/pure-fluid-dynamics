Pure Fluid Dynamics

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

The short version

Pure Fluid Dynamics does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Scenes you save, your quality setting and your wind direction are stored only on your own device, inside the app. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you copy a share link, the scene is encoded inside the link itself. Nothing is uploaded. Screenshots and videos you export go only where you choose to send them through the system share sheet.

Network

The app runs fully offline. It only opens a web page if you tap a link, and that page then opens in your browser.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/pangbom/pure-fluid-dynamics/issues.
