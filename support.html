Support

Getting help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/pangbom/pure-fluid-dynamics/issues. Describe what you did, what you expected and which device you use.

Quick answers

Draw a shape in the wind with Draw. Two fingers rotate and move it. The Speed lever runs the simulation in slow or fast motion without changing the physics. Wind speed, viscosity and friction are in Settings. Tap the circular arrow to restart the flow; hold it to reset the whole scene.

If it runs slowly, choose a lower quality in Settings.

Use it in a browser

The same simulator runs free in any browser at pangbom.github.io/pure-fluid-dynamics.
